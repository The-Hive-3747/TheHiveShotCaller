1. Cameras

Plug in both cameras, then press Detect and preview. Click This is the -X wall camera under the picture taken from the minus-X wall; it becomes cam0 and the other one becomes cam1. This replaces the configured sources.

2. Lens (required unless already measured)

Open printable checkerboard (PDF) Print at 100% / Actual size on a flat, stiff surface.

For each camera: press Start recording, hold the board 1.5 to 3 ft from the camera and tilt it around (left, right, up, down, corners of the picture) for about 20 seconds, then press Stop recording. Green means under 1 px, amber under 2 px, red means redo it.

3. Tags and auto-calibration


Open printable tag sheet (PDF) Print at 100% / Actual size and check the size line.

Place the tags where the sheet says, so both cameras can see at least 2 tags.

4. Verify HIVE tags (optional)

The tags on the up CELL are already known from the manual. This step checks them against what the cameras see and learns the tags on the down CELL, so the HIVE state can then be detected automatically.

Make sure the audience CELL is up. Press Learn. Then tip the HIVE by hand so the far CELL is up, wait until it settles, press Learn again.

5. Ball colors

Place a ball in view, choose its type, then click the ball in the picture.
