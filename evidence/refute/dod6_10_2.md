## What I did (reproduction)

Simulated rig (`simlib.py`): truth cameras from `hiveshot.placement.recommend("usb")` (95 degree HFOV unless stated), frames rendered with the kit tags 100 to 103 (white quiet zone, via `cv2.aruco.generateImageMarker` warped onto the 3D tag quads, base tags leaning back), the HIVE's own CELL tags (`synth._draw_hive_tags`), the frame wireframe, one ball; optional lens distortion; Gaussian noise plus mp4v compression; written as two 1080p mp4s and configured as `file` sources (loop, realtime). No `_truth.json` next to them, so the dev-convenience fixture calibration cannot interfere.

Driver (`run_s.py`) follows the UI's own call order (read from `ui/app.js`): `GET /api/config`, `/api/pipeline/status`, `/api/setup/status`; step 1 `applySources` twice (stop, PUT config, start); step 2 `GET /api/calib/tags`, `/api/calib/tags.pdf`, `POST /api/calib/auto_all {source:"kit"}`; step 3 `POST /api/calib/hive_tags/learn` per state; step 4 `POST /api/detector/sample`; step 5 start pipeline + `POST /api/sessions`; then `POST /api/calib/crosscheck`. Accuracy is measured against the truth cameras, independent of anything the app reports: camera position error, and triangulation error of 316 points of the placement's sample trajectories (shot volume) with 0.3 px noise, plus the CELL opening center. Run one scenario: `HS_ROOT=<snapshot> .venv\Scripts\python.exe evidence\refute\dod6_10_2\run_s.py <scenario> <port>` (scenario names in `run_s.py`; `HS_ROOT` unset uses the live tree). The click-method Monte Carlo is `mc_click.py` (200 trials per noise level, the app's own solver).

I also drove the real UI in the browser pane against the real backend (snapshot, port 8795): Setup tab, step 2 Auto-calibrate, step 3 Learn, step 4 colors, Calibration tab, Cross-check; and the packaged exe (`dist\HiveShotTracker`, built 20:09) on a fresh data dir.

## Findings, ranked

1. **Wizard does not open on a fresh install, and the app claims it is calibrated (DoD 10, high).** Fresh data dir, default config: `GET /api/setup/status` gives `sources_configured: true, calibration_loaded: true`; `/api/calib` returns intrinsics fx 900 from the bundled fixture truth JSON (dev convenience in `pipeline.reload_calibration`). `ui/app.js` init opens Setup only when one of those is false. Screenshot of the packaged exe at first load: Dashboard active, no wizard. README.md ("The setup wizard opens on first run") and operator_guide section 3 ("opens on first run") are false for the exe; the exe does not open a browser either. After step 1 replaces the sources the fake calibration disappears, so the operator who finds the Setup tab gets a correct state.
2. **No intrinsics step in the wizard; green result hides a wrong lens (DoD 10, high).** Wizard steps are Cameras, Tags, Verify HIVE tags, Colors, Done. operator_guide section 3 says "in the order the Setup wizard walks you through it: 1. Intrinsics" but no such step exists. The default intrinsics (fx 771, 103 degree HFOV) disagree with the repo's own placement assumption (95 degree, fx 880), and calibration.md/camera_placement.md say the real lens must be measured. With truth fx 880: wizard "Max error 0.97 in", reprojection 4.2 and 3.7 px (not colored), camera positions 12.2 in off, 3D error up to 7 in. Worse, saving the Auto-calibrate result writes the default intrinsics into calibration.json, and the status warning "uses default intrinsics (arducam_2.3mp_110); calibrate for accuracy" disappears right after Auto-calibrate (status `warnings: []` in every run; also in the browser). Nothing tells the operator the lens was never calibrated.
3. **Guide text refers to controls that do not exist (DoD 6, medium).** calibration.md 3A steps 3, 4, 7 and operator_guide section 3 point 2: the Calibration tab has only Cross-check, Refresh image, Undo point, Clear, Solve extrinsics, Start/Stop intrinsics (checked in the DOM). Auto-calibrate lives in Setup step 2 as one button, and its result is a table below the button, not "the card shows RMSE ... tags used ... reprojection". There is no "Auto-calibrate all" button. operator_guide also says "Crosscheck" and "each camera card shows" (no).
4. **Wizard result labels mislead (medium).** The column "Pose error" is the fit RMSE of the tag corners (`rmse_in`), not pose error: in the 0.5 in taping case it shows 0.29 and 0.22 in while the cameras are 3.6 and 3.3 in off. "Max error" is the out-of-sample cross-check but the wizard never says what was held out, and when only in-sample points exist the backend's `warning` (and `out_of_sample: false`) is dropped by `renderAutoCal`. The reprojection column has no pass/fail color although the guide's limit is 2 px. For the no-printer path with a camera that cannot read HIVE tags the cross-check can be unavailable and the UI prints a red "Max error - in" without a reason. The 1 in threshold cannot see a focal-length error (zoom case 0.80 in green with cameras 8.9 in off) or a one-tag 1 in taping error (0.61 to 0.78 green with 3D p95 above 1 in).
5. **Calibration is saved even when the solve is garbage (medium).** `auto_all` with cam1 at 1280x720 against 1920x1080 intrinsics returned 200 with cam1 reprojection 6.0 px, saved it (cam1 pose 72 in off, 3D error up to 1449 in); only the status warning "resolution mismatch" and the red cross-check (1.80) show it. The previous good calibration is overwritten.
6. **Silent camera swap after calibration (medium).** Calibrate, then swap which device is cam0/cam1 (cable or index shuffle): status stays `calibration_loaded: true`, `warnings: []`, no recommendation; the truth error is 24 to 86 in. Only pressing Cross-check shows it (46 in, red). Re-running Auto-calibrate fixes it (`spot_swap_after.txt`). A swap during setup (wrong wall-camera click) is harmless for the kit path (error unchanged), so the wizard's "-X wall camera" question matters less than it looks.
7. **Tag mounting is not wizard-only (DoD 10, medium).** The wizard lists "Frame crossbar end (-X apex), audience logo panel", "Frame base corner (-X, +Y), standing tilted back 20 deg": no picture of the HIVE, no red/blue/left/right words, and none of the tolerances. The sheet says "measure the placement with a tape to 0.25 in" but not from where; the reference locations (frame center line, panel top edge, foot bar end, 20.25 in) are only in docs/calibration.md. A 20 degree stand has no dimensions anywhere (a foam wedge about 2.05 in rise over 6 in). My sensitivity numbers: 0.5 in errors on all four tags give 3.3 to 3.6 in camera error and 3D 95th percentile 1.9 in; a 3 degree tilt on both base tags is harmless (0.4 in), so the 0.5 degree tilt tolerance in the guide is stricter than needed, but 0.25 in placement is not.
8. **Click fallback accept rule unreachable (DoD 6, medium).** See Monte Carlo. Also points 5 to 8 are the corners of the blue far CELL opening, which faces away from both cameras; the guide asks the operator to click the far rim of a CELL seen from behind (min distance between two points in cam1 is 31 px). I did not model a systematic mis-pick.
9. **Intrinsics step quality depends on board distance (DoD 6, medium, simulation only).** The guide says 2 to 5 ft with a 1.00 in board; at those distances my simulated board gave RMSE 2.2/1.7 px (pinhole) and 3.3/2.7 px (distorted lens), above the guide's 2 px limit, fx 4 to 9 percent low and camera position errors 5 to 10 in. At 1.5 to 3 ft the same code gave RMSE 0.19 to 1.04 px and 0.2 to 1.3 in. The app saves any intrinsics solve with no threshold. Real optics and compression will differ; the point is that the guide's range and its acceptance limit are not consistent.
10. **Wizard step 3 cannot finish, step 4 can poison detection (low to medium).** Step 3 "Learn" with the audience CELL up learns only tags 36 and 37 (both cameras see them) and lists 30 to 35 as "missing" in red; the second half (tip the HIVE so the far CELL is up, learn again) returns 422 "0 HIVE tags seen by both cameras" in every run because the audience-wall cameras cannot see the far CELL's tags; with the far-CELL-up video the state source falls back to manual. The step is labeled optional. Step 4: the preview thumbnails are 320 px wide for a 1920 px frame, so a 20 px ball is about 3 px; clicking a background pixel (tested via the API at u=300, v=300) widens `pollen_hsv` to [[0,0,76],[38,255,255]] permanently ("range only widens"), and the wizard has no undo or reset.
11. **UI WebSocket hardcoded to port 8765 (low, found while testing).** `ui/app.js` line 51 `new WebSocket('ws://127.0.0.1:8765/ws')`. On any other port (operator_guide and troubleshooting both suggest `--port 8766`) the page shows "ws: reconnecting" and the top banner and live counters go stale (after Auto-calibrate the banner still read "no calibration loaded" until reload). Fine on the default port.
12. Smaller: fresh `GET /api/setup/status` takes 2 to 4 s (device probing). Auto-calibrate with only 2 tags per camera succeeds silently (pose error up to 2.4 in) although the guide says it is weak. The kit tag text on the sheet and in the wizard refers to "(-X, +Y)" without saying which end is red.

## What works (so the lead can see what to keep)

On a clean rig whose lens matches the intrinsics the printed-kit path is excellent: 4-tag solve reprojection 0.4 to 0.7 px, camera position error 0.2 in, 3D error over the shot volume mean 0.09 and 95th percentile 0.15 in, CELL opening center within 0.04 in, all well inside the 1 in target; it survives one missing tag (0.2 to 0.4 in), two base tags tilted 3 degrees, and a cam0/cam1 swap at setup. Out-of-sample cross-check now catches a 1 in systematic tape error (1.08 and 1.13 in red), a 2 in single-tag error, a resolution mismatch, a wrong default lens in the no-printer path, and a post-calibration swap. The no-printer path (HIVE tags only) gave 0.7 to 1.3 in camera error when the lens is right. Intrinsics from the checkerboard were accurate (fx within 0.1 percent) when the board was held 1.5 to 3 ft away. All endpoints returned the documented fields; the PDFs serve (letter, 4 pages, 6.00 in tags, size-check line).

## Docs: what a person with no Python still has to know that is not in the UI or the printed sheet

1. That the Setup tab exists and must be opened by hand on a fresh install (README, operator_guide say it opens by itself).
2. That the wizard skips the lens calibration and that the checkerboard (Calibration tab, Start/Stop intrinsics) is needed; the order and the 2 px limit are only in calibration.md.

4. The HIVE vocabulary: crossbar end, apex, logo panel, foot bar, base corner, frame center line. No diagram anywhere.
5. The 0.25 in tolerance reference points and how to build a 20 degree stand and check a tilt.
6. How to read the result: "Pose error" is a fit residual; green "Max error" means held-out HIVE tags, not that the lens is right; reprojection px above 2 means re-measure the tags; what to do on red.
7. That the cameras must already be placed to the 15 to 30 minute procedure in camera_placement.md, and that placement and tape positions changed during this session.
8. That Learn (step 3) cannot complete its second half and that its red "missing" list is expected.
9. That a click on the ball in step 4 must hit a 3 px target, and that a miss cannot be undone from the wizard.
10. The calibration.md "Calibration tab, Auto-calibrate" instruction (does not match the UI).

- The human-time numbers are estimates; I could not time a person.
- The source tree changed during my run (see "Source state tested"); numbers are dated. Re-run `run_s.py` on the final tree.
- The app itself probes device indexes 0 to 5 on `GET /api/setup/status` (the UI polls it every 2 s), so the laptop webcam on index 0 was opened briefly by the app's own probe whenever I or the UI called that endpoint; I did not request any capture or preview from it, and the app never started a pipeline on it.
- I stopped a packaged `HiveShotTracker.exe` once by image name (taskkill) at about 20:15; it was the instance I had started, but that command would have hit any other instance of the exe. Later cleanup used process ids and command-line filters only (one filter also matched and ended my own shell processes).
