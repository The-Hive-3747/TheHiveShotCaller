## DoD 9 "placement map + instructions exist and a fresh setup yields calibration within the M1 target" -> REFUTED

Existence/consistency (NOT refuted): docs/camera_placement.md, .svg, .png exist. Regenerating with placement.write_map('gopro',...) gives byte-identical SVG and PNG (same SHA256). recommend('gopro') and recommend('usb') match the doc table: A (-78,65,36), B (65,78,36), aim (-13,41,61); C gopro (-25,78,58) -> (-12.75,50,30), usb (-13,78,58). Tape distances (7 in along wall; 47/59 in) and pitches (20, 16, ~43/45 deg) recompute correctly. The map only depicts the GoPro option.

Simulation (two VirtualCameras via placement.camera_from_pose at the recommended poses, 1920x1080, 0.3 px Gaussian click noise, calib_targets.field_reference_points, calib.solve_extrinsics_from_points, triangulate the up-CELL opening center from hiveshot.field = (-12.75, 24.249, 59.55); 5 seeds per case):
- Self-consistent world (physical points = the calib_targets sheet): reprojection RMSE 0.07-0.33 px per camera (< 2 px), CELL-center triangulation error 0.02-0.11 in (< 1 in). With 1.0 px noise over 30 seeds: error at probe points (launch, mid-flight, floor, tile) up to 1.9 in worst case, median 0.3-0.9 in. On pure numerics the target is met.
- Only 4 of the 8 reference points are inside the image at the recommended poses. Cam A (both options) sees points 1-4 only; cam B sees 1-4 (gopro) or 1-4 plus 7 (usb). Points 5-8 (HIVE frame base corners, floor tile intersections) fall outside the frame (aim is at z=61, floor is below the bottom edge). calibration.md step 3 ("click the 8 listed points ... spread across the image and include different heights") cannot be followed; the clickable points are the coplanar 20x14 in CELL face. (The solver does work with 4 points, and with all 8 incl. off-image synthetic ones, 0 failures in 120 trials, but a human cannot click off-image points.)
- Inconsistent geometry (key refutation): calib_targets.up_cell_opening_corners() (used by write_field_point_sheet AND by /api/calib/extrinsics/{cam}) puts the opening center at y=15.44 (BEAM_HALF_LENGTH) with the high edge at y=11.93. hiveshot.field.cell_opening_polygon() (used for scoring, viewer and placement) puts the center at y=24.249 (=14/tan 30 deg), high edge at y=20.74. The two differ by 8.8 in at the CELL. If the physical CELL is where field.py says, calibrating with the sheet values triangulates the CELL center 8.77-8.82 in off the field.py value (gopro, 5 seeds); in the usb option cam1's reprojection RMSE also rises to ~3.7-3.9 px (fails < 2 px). If instead the sheet is right, field.py (scoring/viewer) is 8.8 in off. Either way the "known point triangulates within 1 in of its CAD value (hiveshot.field)" check fails by ~8.8 in and nothing cross-tests the two models. (All these constants are marked VERIFY; none measured on a real field.)
- write_field_point_sheet() text says "X toward the audience side" while the project uses +Y toward the audience.

Followability gaps in camera_placement.md
1. It never says which physical camera is cam0 / cam1. The doc uses A/B/C; only operator_guide.md says RTMP cam0 = A, cam1 = B; calibration.md and the placement doc say nothing, and USB device-index to A/B mapping is not stated.
2. Option B contradicts the owner decision: the placement doc (Link row, Cable row, step 6) and operator_guide.md say USB via powered hub; SHOPPING_LIST.md and PURCHASE_LIST.md say each Arducam goes into a separate direct PC port, no hub (two 1080p90 MJPEG streams starve on one hub link). The placement doc does not state the direct-port requirement (it says "different USB root port or the powered hub").
3. The aim point (-13,+41,+61) is a 3-D point in the air in field coordinates; an operator cannot aim at it without software. Usable guidance is only "CELL opening about 17 in in front of its lip ... check by eye" and a pitch angle. Yaw (compass direction) is never given; no tolerance; no sight-line recipe (taped X, laser, preview overlay).
4. Orientation ambiguity: "-X wall", "+Y wall", "corner (-72,+72)" are defined only by the map convention (X right, Y up, audience at top). Viewed from the audience side that appears mirrored. The doc never defines X/Y with physical landmarks (e.g. "standing on the audience side facing the field, -X is on your right") or says which physical HIVE is red / x=-12.75.
5. "6 in out from the wall" with "wall = +-72": not stated whether 72 is the inside face, outside face or tile edge, nor what is measured (foot center, lens center, tripod head).
6. "Roll level: the horizon (wall top) must be horizontal" is unusable when the wall is out of frame (cameras look at z=61).
7. Step 7 says both beams and the launch area must be in frame in A and B, but per the sim the floor/launch-line references are off-frame; per-camera visibility of reference points is undocumented.
8. The "fresh setup" calibration steps depend on calibration.md (Python-API prose, checkerboard print and intrinsics first) and on VERIFY-flagged CAD constants.
9. Option B's 95 deg HFOV is an admitted assumption with no step to measure it before extrinsics.
10. Expected-numbers table (ball pixels, angles) not recomputed by me.

## Verdicts
- DoD 8: REFUTED as worded (no clean machine; a real risk in the missing plain msvcp140.dll; zip lacks the docs). Functional behavior in a stripped-env same-machine simulation: NOT refuted.
- DoD 9: REFUTED. Numerics at the recommended poses are fine (< 0.35 px, < 0.12 in at 0.3 px noise), but (a) the calibration point sheet disagrees with the app's field model by 8.8 in at the CELL so the "within 1 in of CAD" criterion fails against hiveshot.field; (b) 4 of 8 reference points are not visible from the recommended poses so the procedure is not followable as written; (c) the placement instructions have the gaps above (cam0/cam1, hub vs direct USB, unachievable aim point, orientation).

Suggested fixes: one shared CELL geometry for calib_targets and field.py with an equality test; choose reference points visible from the recommended poses (or document CELL-face-only calibration); state cam0/cam1 and the direct-USB rule in the placement doc; give yaw bearings or a floor-mark aiming recipe; define X/Y physically; ship docs in the zip; run a genuine clean-VM / second-PC test.
