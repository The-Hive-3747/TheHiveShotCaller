# Refutation report dod9_5 (DoD 9, round 5)

Claim: "A camera placement map and placement instructions exist in the project folder, and a fresh setup following them yields calibration within the M1 accuracy target" (reprojection under 2 px per camera; the up-CELL opening center triangulates within 1 in of its CAD value).

**VERDICT: NOT REFUTED.** The round-4 defect (the blue audience CELL hiding kit tag 100 in the match-start state) is fixed by the documented calibration state, and I could not find an occlusion, a numeric inconsistency or a simulated failure that breaks the claim. What I found are weaknesses (section 6): a printed-sheet/usability gap, a wedge-placement ambiguity, a few doc inconsistencies, optimistic click pass rates (by about 5 to 8 points), and the usual limits of a synthetic test (true intrinsics, no real detector images). Nothing changes a pass into a fail.

All work is under `evidence/refute/dod9_5/` (source read-only; no installs, no servers, no git writes). Run as `PYTHONPATH=. .venv\Scripts\python.exe evidence\refute\dod9_5\<script>` from the project root. `tests/test_placement.py tests/test_tagkit.py tests/test_calib.py` run with no failure printed (project addopts `-q` hides the summary line).

## 3. Simulation of the documented procedure: NOT REFUTED

`sim5.py` (outputs `sim5_usb|gopro.txt/json`, `sim5_big.py` + `sim5_big_usb|gopro.txt/json`). Single process. World = CALIBRATION_STATE: kit tags 100-103 (cards drawn with the real 0.5 in white border, exact 3-D card quad), HIVE tags 34-41 on both up CELLs, 1920x1080, 2 gray-level noise, 3x3 blur. True cameras at the documented spots with uniform placement error +-1 in x/y, +-0.5 in lens, +-2 deg yaw/pitch, +-1 deg roll. Tag mounting error 0.25 in (random direction) and 0.5 deg (random axis). A tag is dropped if my hull test (above) hides it (never happened: 0 runs). Intrinsics: true. Two kit-solve paths: `app` = `calib.solve_extrinsics_from_tags` on the rendered image (the app path), and `n0.5` = detected corners plus 0.5 px Gaussian noise. Gates: reprojection < 2 px in BOTH cameras, and the triangulated up-CELL opening center (0.5 px noise in each projection) within 1 in of the CAD value.

Kit solve (source kit), both gates. Documented tolerance, 230 seeds (30 + 200 disjoint):

| Option | app path both gates | n0.5 path both gates | opening center < 1 in | opening center err med / p99 / max | out-of-sample (HIVE tags, max corner err) < 1 in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arducam | **230/230** | 223/230 (97 %) | 230/230 | 0.32 / 0.66 / 0.84 (n0.5: max 0.77) | 228/230 (max 1.09) |
| GoPro | **229/230** | 225/230 (98 %) | 230/230 | 0.35 / 0.74 / 0.87 (n0.5: max 0.96) | 228/230 (max 1.15) |

Also (30 seeds): exact tags 30/30 and 30/30 for both options and both paths (opening center max 0.28 in); documented tolerance plus the base cards 0.2 in in front of their model position (foam-core thickness): app 30/30 both options, n0.5 30/30 Arducam, 29/30 GoPro; cards tilted +-1.5 deg about their bottom edge (a hand-cut wedge: +-0.05 in on a 2 in dimension is +-1.4 deg): 29-30/30 per row, opening center max 0.69 in (`wedge_tilt.py`). Per-camera numbers (200-seed row): reprojection median 1.0 to 1.4 px, max 2.15 (n0.5) / 2.04 (app); camera position error median 0.9 to 2.2 in, max 6.5 in (GoPro A has only tags 100, 101, 103, 3 tags; Arducam A 4 tags in 57 % of runs, else 3); tags used min 3; rotation error up to 3.7 deg. The pose is several inches off along the viewing direction, and still the opening center is within 1 in in every run, because the pose error is nearly collinear with the rays that triangulate it. App-displayed `rmse_in`: median 0.10 to 0.14 in, max 0.21 in at 0.25 in mounting (60 seeds per option, `rmse_in.txt`), so the doc's second accept rule (RMSE under 0.25 in) is met too.

Out-of-sample caveat: in the synthetic render camera A decodes the HIVE tags (the doc says it cannot at 60 deg), so the held-out check here is optimistic for A (the doc says it falls back to in-sample).

Sensitivity to the intrinsics the app will actually hold (`intr_sens.py`): common focal-length error 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 % and principal point +-4 px (Gaussian), 40 seeds each, both options: opening center < 1 in 40/40 in every row (max 0.83 in); reprojection < 2 px in both cameras 36-40/40 (the principal point error alone costs about 1-2 runs in 40). So a 1 to 2 % focal error does not break the target here; the doc's own warning that a grossly wrong lens does is separate.

Failure modes: a card stuck 180 deg rotated gives reprojection 33 to 55 px, which the app refuses (> 4 px) (`rot180.py`); the doc's 1.5 to 3 px advice does not cover that case.

Click method (8 CELL corners, coplanar), gate RMSE < 2 px and no point > 3 px, placement error as above (`click_many5.py`, 300 seeds per row; and the 30-seed and 200-seed runs inside `sim5`):

| noise per axis | Arducam per camera | GoPro per camera | both cameras on the first try (Arducam / GoPro) | opening center < 1 in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.7 px | 99 / 98 % | 99 / 97 % | 96 / 97 % | 300/300 |
| 1.0 px | 88 / 84 % | 89 / 83 % | 73 / 74 % | 300/300 |
| 1.5 px | **39 / 41 %** (1 solver exception in 300) | **45 / 42 %** | **19 / 21 %** | 299/299 and 300/300 (max 0.87 in) |

`sim5`'s own 1.5 px rows: 159/400 and 162/400 per camera (40 %), both cameras on the first try 29/200 and 33/200. The documented pass rate at 1.5 px is "46 to 48 %" (calibration.md 3B), so the doc is about 5 to 8 points optimistic (about 2 standard errors for one option; it is consistent for 0.7 and 1.0 px: doc 198/199 and 179/175 of 200 against my 98-99 % and 83-89 %). I also saw `solve_extrinsics_from_points` raise "PnP RANSAC failed: too few consistent points" in 1 of 300 Arducam camera-solves at 1.5 px (the app would show an error, the operator redoes the clicks). At ordinary clicking, expect to redo a camera about 3 times in 5 and the pair about 4 times in 5; the pose that passes is always good (opening center < 1 in in all 1800+ solves). Fallback only; NOT REFUTED as such.

## 4. Physical plausibility: NOT REFUTED (with an ambiguity)

- **20 deg wedge and the 7 in card (`wedge_fit.py`).** Black square 6.00 in plus 0.5 in border = 7.00 in card; wedge 2 in deep x 5.5 in tall, slope face 5.85 in, angle 19.98 deg. The card is glued to the slope, so it does not "stand on" the wedge: card centre of mass (y 19.06, z 3.29) is inside the base footprint (y 18.25..20.25), so it is stable by itself; the card overhangs the wedge by 1.15 in of slope length at the top, no problem. The card back also touches the foot-bar-end top edge (y 19.47, z 2.15), which is where the 20 deg lean comes from (20.25 - 2.15 tan 20 = 19.47).
- **Wedge versus the foot bar.** If the wedge fills only the 4 in of the card inboard of the bar (x 18.73..22.75, the `tagkit.py` comment) it touches nothing (0.00 in3 overlap with the bar and the leg foot, 0.55 in from the leg). If it is centered on the card or on the black square (x 19.73..23.73, what "glue the back of the card to the sloped face" suggests) it overlaps the foot bar by 2.6 in3 and the leg foot by 0.5 in3; flush with the outer face: 5.2 and 0.9 in3. calibration.md section 0 step 4b does **not** say where along the card the wedge goes; the kit figure says "the wedge may be notched where it crosses the foot bar end", and the doc says "the wedge may ride on top of the foot bar end behind the card", which cannot be done while the card bottom stays on the tile at 20.25 (the bar ends at 19.47, the wedge is 2 in deep). Followable (only the card position counts and the doc says so), but three texts disagree.
- **"Cut on the dashed line" on the printed sheet.** `write_tag_sheet_pdf` (`sheet/tags.pdf`, 4 letter pages, `sheet/p3_small.png`): the dashed rectangle is on every page, 0.50 in outside the black square (pixel check: x 0.743..7.757 in on the top row against 0.75..7.75 expected, 3 px lines); each page says "Cut exactly on the dashed line (0.5 in white border)", base-tag pages also "Cut ON the dashed line". Present. But the tag number, the frame-feature name and the "THIS EDGE UP" arrow are printed outside the dashed line, so they are cut away. Only the line "Label up: the arrow points to the top edge." (small text, 0.35 in above the black square, inside the card's top white border) stays on the card. calibration.md 3A step 1 says the arrow makes "the printed TAG number and name read upright"; after cutting nothing but the AprilTag pattern identifies the card, and the orientation cue is a sentence about an arrow that is gone. A mixed-up or rotated card fails safe (reprojection 30+ px, solve refused, section 3), but nothing in the docs tells the operator to write the id and an "up" mark on the card before cutting. Detection with the real printed card (page PNG warped onto the projected card, text in the border included) is unchanged against the clean marker: same decode counts, reprojection median 0.50-0.53 vs 0.55-0.57 px (`printed_card.py`).
- **Tags 100/101 on the panel (`panel_fit.py`).** On `field.logo_panel_face("audience")` plane coordinates the 6 in squares at x +-10 have all corners on the plane (0.0003 in) and inside the trapezoid: top edge flush (margin 0), margin to the slanted side edge 0.215 in at the top outer corner and 1.45 in at the lower outer corner, 0.457 in above the bottom edge. They fit. The 7 in card hangs 0.5 in over the top edge and 0.28 in over the top corner (and 0.04 in over the bottom edge). Against the CAD top corner block (face at y 1.52) the card's top border, whose plane is at y 1.463 there, would run 0.06 in into the block over x -13.5..-10.5 (tag 100): irrelevant in practice (a 0.06 in flexing of the card edge, about 0.5 deg of tilt if rigid), noted as the only geometric conflict I found.
- Frame, legs: the base cards sit 1.9 in in front of the leg (leg centerline y 17.5 at z 3, black square at y 19.2), nothing interferes.

## 6. Weaknesses that do not change the verdict

1. Synthetic world only: pinhole camera with the true intrinsics, true lens centers, no lens distortion; the detector sees rendered, not real, frames (the doc itself says so for readability, "VERIFY with the first footage"). Intrinsics sensitivity (focal 2 %, principal point 4 px) kept 40/40 openings under 1 in.
2. The 1 in target depends on the 0.25 in / 0.5 deg tag-mounting tolerance (the doc's own table: 14/30 at 0.5 in / 1 deg; 0/30 at 1 in / 2 deg). Achieving 0.25 in on a card leaning on a foot-bar edge is on the operator; the simulation shows the pose is not sensitive to a 0.2 in card thickness or +-1.5 deg wedge error.
3. The out-of-sample check cannot be independent for camera A (doc says so); in my synthetic frames A does decode the HIVE tags, so that column is optimistic.
4. The click method passes its own gate at ordinary click noise only about 40 % per camera (doc 46-48 %), pairs about 20 %.
5. GoPro camera A has only 3 kit tags (102 cut off), so its camera-position error reaches 6.5 in; the opening center stays under 1 in (max 0.87 in), margin 0.13 in at the worst seed (n0.5 path, 30 seeds, 0.96 in).
